The PV bike bus is rolling

A group of neighborhood kids and grown-ups riding bikes to school together, on a set route, at a set time. Safer than riding alone, way more fun than the car line.

Join the ride See the route

What it is

Just like a school bus - except everybody's rolling

Riders join in along a fixed route at set stops, and we all ride in together as a group, led by volunteers.

01

Safety in numbers

Riding as a visible group is safer than riding solo — drivers see us coming, and adult volunteers ride at the front and back.

02

Skip the car line

No idling, no parking scramble. Kids roll straight up to the bike rack, wide awake and ready for the day.

03

Genuinely fun

Music, bubbles, decorated bikes, and friends the whole way. Most kids ask to ride again the next day.

Questions

Good to know before your first ride

Is there a waiver?

By choosing to participate in the Bike Bus, you agree to our terms and liability waiver.

What are the rules?

Please review our rules of the road.

Do we have to ride the whole route?

No - join at whichever stop is closest to your house and ride the rest of the way with the group.

What if it's raining/snowing?

We ride rain or shine; slippery conditions excepted. Watch the group email for updates.

Does a grown-up need to ride with my kid?

All ages are welcome! However, K-1st graders need a parent/guardian accompanying them. Guardians are welcome but not required for older riders.

How do I volunteer?

We always need extra adults riding front, middle, and back. Email to join. No experience needed — just a bike, a helmet, and a hi-vis vest (we've got spares).
