Rules of the Road

Guidelines for riders, volunteers, and caregivers to keep everyone safe while we ride together.

Safety first

Basic rules for riders

  • Stop at all stop signs and obey traffic controls; look both ways before proceeding.
  • Stay behind the lead adults and follow their instructions.
  • When crossing major roads (for example 32nd Ave, Kipling), wait for lead volunteers to give the OK, and cross together as a group.
  • Ride on the right-hand side of the road in a single or double line.
  • Helmets are strongly recommended for all riders.
  • For a full review of rules, please see Bicycle Colorado's Rules of the Road.

Participation requirements

  • Kids in 2nd grade and older should be confident riders who understand basic road safety (stopping, looking, signaling).
  • Kindergarten and 1st grade riders should have a parent or guardian ride alongside them.
  • By participating you agree to our terms and liability waiver.

Volunteer expectations

  • Volunteers lead at the front and will bring up the rear. This keeps the group together and safe.
  • Additional volunteers can also ride with the group.
  • Bring a basic repair kit and a mobile phone for emergencies when possible.
  • Keep an eye out for unexpected hazards.
More details and common questions

If you have specific accessibility needs, medical concerns, or questions about a route, contact the organizers before the ride so we can accommodate you safely.
